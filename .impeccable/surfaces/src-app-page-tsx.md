Scope: the whole game (crate home, play, back-cover results). Mode: Experience for play; Operate for crate and results.
Audience/job: music lovers, once a day on a phone; type the rarest correct answer to six prompts, then share.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every daily set is a new LP. The prompt is the front cover, set huge in stacked grotesque on one flat color field; the results are the back cover with a tracklist and liner notes. This rejects the category default of a dark quiz card plus a progress bar.

OWN-WORLD: Flat offset-ink fields with no shadows, gradients, or glass, depth only from overlapping fields. Warm ink ground (#16130F) and cream paper (#F3ECDD). Each pack owns one field color: General ochre, Hip-Hop vermilion, Pop cobalt. Archivo at extreme widths (condensed 900 stacked against expanded) for display, Libre Franklin for liner-note small print, catalog numbers and tabular numerals. Buttons are flat ink blocks; the input is a title line underlined into the cover.

STORY: Pick a sleeve from the crate, play six tracks against a 30-second clock (which can be turned off), and see that the obscure answer outscored the hit. Flip to the back cover, share the tracklist, come back tomorrow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Play, mobile. A square color-field sleeve owns the top half: the catalog line in small print, "Name a" small, then the subject in condensed 900 at maximum size. Below on the ink ground sit the answer line, the Dig block, and fixed slots for track number, clock, and score.

FORM: Hard-Bop Sleeve, my #5 of 7. Signature: on submit, a vinyl disc slides out from under the sleeve, its center label printed with the points and tier. Raises: fixed slots (seven-segment), overlap-only depth (papercut), one monumental prompt per sleeve (linocut), one label voice per pack (night market). seed:89847a710ce0951c

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
